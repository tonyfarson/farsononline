Whitepaper

Hierarchical Epistemological AI Layering

The first generation of large language models proved the concept works. They also built on a structurally flawed foundation — epistemic flattening, where peer-reviewed research and a Reddit thread carry identical weight in the training pipeline. A market correction is coming. HEAL is a design specification for the architecture that comes after it.

The framework organizes AI knowledge into four stratified tiers with distinct governance rules, confidence metadata, and modification standards. The result: an AI that knows what it knows, knows what it thinks, and knows what it prefers — and is architecturally required to communicate the difference.
